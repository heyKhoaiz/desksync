Notes in Chrome, one shortcut away

Press Alt+Shift+K on any page and start typing. Checklists, email drafts and saved replies, kept in your browser. No account, no limit on notes.

Add to Chrome, free Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers

Formatting you can see

Bold, italic, lists and checklists show as they look while you write. Underneath, the note is plain text, so nothing is locked in.

Checklists that keep count

Start a note as Tasks, or type /task. Tick items off and the list shows how far along you are.

Keep a quote with its source

Select text, right-click, Save selection as a note. The page's address goes at the foot of the note.

Saved replies for any text box

Click into a text box, open DeskSync, pick a note and press Shift+Enter. Gmail, Docs and Notion get the formatting; plain fields get the words.

Drafts that open in your mail

Start a note as Email, add To and Subject, and Open in mail fills in a new message in your mail app or Gmail.

Find it from the address bar

Type ds, a space and a word. One search covers your notes, saved pages and copied text.

Your notes stay in your browser

  • Saved on your computer first. Notes work offline and need no account.
  • Nothing leaves your browser unless you sign in to sync.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • Saves as you type. Close the popup mid-sentence and the note is still there.
